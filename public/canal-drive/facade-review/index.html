Façade review — blind labelling

Read each façade and record what you see. The detector's answer is deliberately not shown: a reviewer asked "the detector says four storeys, is that right?" agrees far more often than one asked how many storeys there are, and that difference is the bias this corpus exists to measure. Answers are kept in this browser until you export them.

Storeys above ground

Count window bands, not the roof. A souterrain half below the pavement does not count; a raised bel-étage does.

Window bays across the front

Vertical columns of windows. A single wide shopfront is one bay.
