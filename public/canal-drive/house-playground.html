How close is this to the game?

Same: the decoration chain (citywide priors, generic facade, monument gables, roof planner, shopfront and landmark steps), the chunk builders (walls, roofs, facade extras), the facade shader and texture arrays for each look.

Different: no MapLibre basemap (a flat ground and street strip), no street graph (the generator gives the terrace one street in front, real buildings put doors on any outer wall), chunks built on the main thread, and the camera is a free orbit instead of the game's chase view.
