Credits & data licences

Ride Amsterdam rebuilds the city from open data, plus original flat-colour geometry drawn by the project. Nothing here is traced from a commercial map or a stock photo library. This page lists the sources and their licences; where a source asks for attribution, the credit also appears in the game.

Map and geometry data

  • OpenStreetMap contributors — canals, streets, bridges, parks, public transport lines, building footprints and landmark placement coordinates. Licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL). © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • BAG (Basisregistratie Adressen en Gebouwen) and BGT (Basisregistratie Grootschalige Topografie) — building footprints, addresses, construction years, ground cover and street names, via Kadaster and PDOK. CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  • 3DBAG — 3D building massing, roof shapes and heights, from TU Delft 3D geoinformation. CC BY 4.0. docs.3dbag.nl.
  • AHN (Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland) — terrain and surface heights. CC0 1.0. ahn.nl.
  • Gemeente Amsterdam open data — data.amsterdam.nl: neighbourhood and district boundaries, street-name register records, the municipal tree inventory and other datasets published as open data. The buurt/gebied data is CC0; the tree inventory is published as “openbaar, tenzij anders aangegeven” (open unless stated otherwise).
  • PDOK aerial imagery — orthophoto and colour-infrared reference used to study facades and roofs. CC BY 4.0.

Street panoramas and photographs (reference only)

  • Gemeente Amsterdam street panoramas — via data.amsterdam.nl, CC BY 4.0. Used to measure real facades, roofs, signs and street furniture. The models are original flat-colour geometry; no panorama pixels are shipped.
  • Stadsarchief Amsterdam Beeldbank — archief.amsterdam/beeldbank: historical photographs, referenced by catalogue number and used only where the item is licensed. Each item keeps its own rights statement.
  • Wikimedia Commons — reference photographs for landmark, bridge and sculpture reconstructions. Each file carries its own licence (commonly CC0 / public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA); the author and licence for each model are recorded in the model catalogue and on the in-game card for that landmark. No photograph pixels and no third-party meshes are shipped.
  • Neighbourhood photographs — the in-game neighbourhood postcards and the neighbourhood pages show Wikimedia Commons photographs loaded from Commons, each with its author and licence under the picture and a link to its file page.

Text and facts

  • Wikipedia — landmark and street summaries, used under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each card links to its own article (“Read the full article on Wikipedia”), and text translated from Dutch is marked as translated.
  • Wikidata — identifiers and cross-references for bridges and landmarks. CC0. wikidata.org.
  • RCE national monuments register — descriptions of listed buildings (rijksmonumenten). CC0.

Basemap

  • The optional vector basemap uses OpenStreetMap data (ODbL) served by OpenFreeMap, styled with the OpenMapTiles schema. The same attribution is shown in the map corner during play.

Models, icons and other assets

  • Source code — the game, the building generators and the models are on GitHub: github.com/blackmad/map-recall2. Contributions welcome; start with the building guide.
  • Building models — the landmark, ordinary-building and block-face models you can download from the building explorer are released under CC BY 4.0: credit “Ride Amsterdam”, and keep the source credits — 3DBAG (TU Delft 3D geoinformation, CC BY 4.0), BAG (Kadaster) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). The models are produced works from those sources; the source databases keep their own licences.
  • Icons — the Route and Waves icons are from Lucide (ISC licence); the Bridge icon is from Font Awesome Free (CC BY 4.0).
  • Bicycles — the default omafiets and the Swapfiets-style bike are authored by the project; the pink city bicycle is Kin Chen’s “Pink city bicycle”, released CC0 via MorfVision / BlenderKit.
  • Boat — the canal sloep was generated by the project with Meshy AI.
  • Trees and planting — crown shapes, foliage and bark colours are original approximations, not surveyed crowns. Positions, species and (where recorded) planting years come from the municipal tree inventory and OpenStreetMap.
  • Transit mode currently shows a placeholder metro mesh; it is not part of the credited asset set and is not final.
