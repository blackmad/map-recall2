Sluisbuurt

The Sluisbuurt is a district, partly still under construction, on Zeeburgereiland in Amsterdam-Oost. The district lies by the Oranjesluizen, after which it is named, and the Binnen-IJ, and covers the area between the Zuiderzeeweg and the Schellingwouderbrug and the IJburglaan towards the Piet Heintunnel.
