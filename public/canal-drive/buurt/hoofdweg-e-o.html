Hoofdweg e.o.

The Hoofdweg is a street in Amsterdam-West running from the Surinameplein to the Bos en Lommerplein, lined mostly with Amsterdam School housing blocks. Wikipedia (translated from Dutch)

The name. The name matches Hoofdweg, in the area. The former municipality of Sloten gave the name for its function: a wide central main road (hoofdweg). After the annexation of 1921 the road was extended north to the Bos en Lommerplein. Gemeente Amsterdam street-name register

Did you know

Trivia the ride can show you here.

  • Hoofdweg simply means ‘main road’: Sloten named it in 1919 for its new districts west of the Kostverlorenvaart. Wikipedia (translated from Dutch)
  • Amsterdam annexed Sloten in 1921 and kept the name; the road reached the Bos en Lommerplein in 1948. Wikipedia (translated from Dutch)
