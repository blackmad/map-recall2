Neighbourhood in Houthaven

Revaleiland

On Revaleiland the closed building blocks show the Amsterdam School style. The Houthaven's seven car-free islands are named after Baltic ports and each was given its own character. PPHP architects, Houthaven (translated from Dutch)

The name. Now Tallinn, the capital of Estonia. In the seventeenth century Amsterdam was the largest buyer of its rye, grain and timber. Gemeente Amsterdam street-name register
