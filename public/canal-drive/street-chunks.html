Each block face of recipe houses is drawn in the game as one mesh instead of one model per house (the game default; ?streetChunks=0 turns it off). Left: the individual house models as they are installed. Right: the chunk. Both use the same orbit camera and move together: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Numbers come from the build report (scripts/street-chunks/build.ts); the fidelity table is scripts/street-chunks/gates.ts.
