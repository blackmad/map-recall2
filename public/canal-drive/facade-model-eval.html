Façade model comparison — Amsterdam

Seven models on the same rectified crops, scored against point-cloud-measured openings in wall metres. The rule was fixed before any model ran: recall ≥ 0.80, precision ≥ 0.80, median centre error ≤ 0.30 m. Decision D1 is the union of RF-DETR and YOLO26x-seg, with doors winning over overlapping windows.
