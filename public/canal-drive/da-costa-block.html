FIELD NOTES

What makes this block itself?

Exact building footprints and reconstructed roofs carry the shape. Public-space data adds the street. A few observed shopfronts and simplified façade families add character.

Window counts, balcony distribution, materials, furniture and canopy shapes are illustrative. Municipal records and photographs have different dates; this is a recognition study, not a survey of today's street works.
