How this experiment works

Front-facing wall surfaces receive the facade artwork. Roofs and unseen sides remain source geometry. Texture alignment is an experimental registration; generated openings and hidden details are not surveyed measurements.

Relief and raised-frame candidates are derived locally from the drawing. They demonstrate rendering options, not a verified building reconstruction.

Prompts for the next pipeline

Colour pass: preserve fixed facade boundaries and opening anchors; remove foreground objects; produce an unlit, shadow-free colour texture with no outlines or baked reflections.

Feature pass: identify windows, doors, frames, storefronts and balconies in image pixel coordinates. Return confidence and visibility for each. Keep source coordinates; do not redraw the building.

Relief pass: use approved opening masks to assign shallow frame/reveal depths. Keep depth separate from colour; infer no new openings.

These are proposed follow-up prompts. This demo uses the three existing Pro drawings and local feature extraction.
