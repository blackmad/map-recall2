Prototype assumptions

This is an image-space reconstruction study. Display width is 38 m; rear depth is 3 m; opening recesses are 0.16–0.25 m and balcony projection is 0.65 m. These are estimates, not surveyed dimensions or verified BAG placement.

Openings use rectangular approximations; arches, round windows and roof beams need further work. Balcony rails use procedural spacing. Tentative text includes the partially hidden inscription and blurred shop name. Lettering placement was transferred manually to the generated image.
