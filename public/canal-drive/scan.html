Kit

App: Scaniverse (free, by Niantic). It uses the iPhone's LiDAR, keeps real-world scale, processes on the phone and exports GLB with no subscription. Polycam is the fallback; its LiDAR mode is as good, but some exports need a paid plan.

  1. New scan, choose Mesh (not Splat), size Medium for a statue, Large for a monument.
  2. Walk a full slow circle about 1–2 m away, once at chest height and once holding the phone high, angled down at the head and top. Include a strip of ground; it gives us the level and the plinth.
  3. Overcast light is best. Avoid low sun across the statue and people standing in the scan.
  4. Process with Detail, then Share → Export → GLB.
  5. Name the file with the code on the card (tap "Copy code") and drop it in the iCloud Drive folder Ride scans. Also save 3–4 normal photos from the front. I pick the folder up, clean the mesh and build the game version.
