# The crossing register

Every vertical crossing the ground gets wrong by inference gets an evidenced record: where a deck, a tunnel or a
level crossing really is, how high, and which ways it binds. The game treats reviewed records as truth. Lane G3 of the
Ground v2 plan (`docs/handoffs/ground-v2-plan-20261110.md`) built it on 2026-11-10; `railViaducts.ts`'s Droogbak
record is the precedent it generalises.

## Files

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `src/canalRecall/ownGround/crossings.ts` | Schema (`CrossingRecord`, `CrossingRuntime`), validation, `runtimeOf`, `crossingIndex` (what the game takes), `floorCaps` (G1's input) |
| `src/canalRecall/ownGround/crossingFiles.ts` | The authored files (`CROSSING_FILES`, `AUTHORED`) for scripts and tests; never imported by the game |
| `data/crossings/runtime.json` | The compact extract the game bundles (33 KB, 8 KB gzip): reviewed records, the fields `crossingIndex`/`floorCaps` read, coordinates to 1e-6°; written by `compose.ts` (and `--runtime`), pinned equal to `runtimeOf(AUTHORED)` by the test |
| `src/canalRecall/ownGround/crossings.test.ts` | Schema, register files, spans/cuts/portals behaviour, byte-identity with no record |
| `data/crossings/<area>.json` | The authored register, generated by `compose.ts` (piers, tunnels, binds, evidence: 7.4 MB, not bundled) |
| `data/crossings/review/<area>.json` | The review decisions (hand-edited): drafts merged per record, kind, portals, notes, verdict |
| `scripts/crossings/draft.ts` | Draft records for an area from BGT, OSM and AHN, with a disagreement report |
| `scripts/crossings/review-sheet.ts` | Orthophoto + panorama sheet per record (`--game` overlays the inferred cuts, mouths and spans) |
| `scripts/crossings/compose.ts` | Review decisions + drafts → the register (rail-deck axes measured from OSM) |
| `scripts/crossings/areas.ts` | Areas: `named`, `ring-rail`, `a10`, `tunnels` |
| `scripts/crossings/sources.ts` | Cached fetchers: BGT OGC API, AHN WCS, Overpass, city panoramas (`artifacts/crossings/raw`) |

Every script prints its usage with `--help`.

## Workflow

```sh
npx tsx scripts/crossings/draft.ts --area ring-rail [--game]       # artifacts/crossings/drafts/ring-rail.json + -report.json
npx tsx scripts/crossings/review-sheet.ts --file artifacts/crossings/drafts/ring-rail.json --game --out artifacts/crossings/review-drafts
# look at every sheet; write data/crossings/review/ring-rail.json (one decision per record, notes from the photos)
npx tsx scripts/crossings/compose.ts --area ring-rail               # unreviewed register (no sheet yet for the final ids)
npx tsx scripts/crossings/review-sheet.ts --file data/crossings/ring-rail.json --game --out artifacts/crossings/review
npx tsx scripts/crossings/compose.ts --area ring-rail               # now reviewed: the sheet's ortho and panorama ids are the evidence
```

Add a new area file to `CROSSING_FILES` and `RUNTIME_AREAS` in `crossingFiles.ts`; `compose.ts` rewrites `runtime.json` after every area. Only records with `reviewed` set act in the game.

## Kinds and what the game does

| Kind | Spans (railSpans.ts) | Cuts (tunnels.ts) | Mouths |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rail-over-road`, `road-over-road`, `road-over-rail` | inferred ones in the zone go; rail/metro/tram decks become spans for tracks along `axisDeg` | stations in the zone go | none |
| `road-under` | inferred ones in the zone go | kept | only those within 15 m of a listed portal |
| `level`, `none` | inferred ones in the zone go | stations in the zone go | none |

`game.cuts` / `game.portals` override the defaults per record. The register is off by default on main since 2026-11-12
(ffe04dd5d, the Kimpoweg pin); `?crossings=1` turns it on. With no reviewed record in a cell the build is byte-identical.

A record's rail deck makes a span for a track only where the track's bed (railProfile level) is nearer the record's
deck top than its street (`spans(..., levelAt)`): a tram at street level under a metro viaduct gets none.

Caps are not applied at runtime here: `floorCaps()` hands G1 the deck polygons and street levels, and G2c's
`crossingConstraints(record)` adapter maps the record onto `GroundConstraint`s. Field names for that adapter:
`plan.decks` (the design's `plan.deck`), `plan.abutments`, `plan.tunnels`, `plan.portals`, `heights.streetUnder`
(`heights.under`), `heights.gradeSides` (`heights.grade`), `binds.over` / `binds.under` (`ways`).

## Pins

`npx tsx scripts/own-ground/check-ground-cells.ts --crossings` checks every reviewed named-place record against the
game's build (and prints the before, `crossings: false`). It also runs in the default `check-ground-cells.ts` pass.

Rail-over-road pins require no inferred span off the record's rail decks and no cut or mouth in the zone; they do not
require a span on the deck, because the ring tracks over the named places are OSM bridge ways (a1's rails lane: the
Kranenbergpad and the Arlandabrug are level crossings with no rail span).

The other records (ring decks at the Kranenbergpad, Amstelveenseweg and Schinkel; the A10 south abutment at the

## Ring railway (2026-11-12)

`data/crossings/ring-rail.json`: 14 reviewed records from 66 drafts (Sloterdijk – Lelylaan – Zuid – RAI; the named
places are not repeated). `data/crossings/review/ring-rail.json` keeps the 11 rejected groups with the reason
(`rejected`): mostly footbridges over water the drafter took for crossings, one BGT deck that covers a whole block
at Sloterdijk, and two Zuidasdok structures under construction. The game infers no span, cut or mouth inside any
ring-rail zone, before or after, so these records change nothing at runtime; they are G1's cap input. Every reviewed
record, in every area, is pinned by `check-ground-cells.ts --crossings`.

## A10 (2026-11-12)

`data/crossings/a10.json`: 11 reviewed records from 54 drafts. 22 of the drafts lay inside named or ring-rail zones;
17 groups were rejected, with reasons kept in the review file. The records cover the A10's decks at Sloterdijk and the
Coen junction, the Henk Sneevlietweg junction, the Vlaardingenlaan west slip, the Amstel bridge, the Kabelweg, the
Bos en Lommerweg, the Wiltzanghlaan, the Solitudolaan path, and the Kruislaan under the Gooilijn.
Only one of them changes the runtime: `kruislaan-under-gooilijn`. The Gooilijn tracks there are not OSM bridge ways,
so the deck becomes their rail span (2 spans, clearance from the estimated soffit). The Kruislaan's real sunken slot
stays (`game: { cuts: 'keep', portals: 'keep' }`). The pins honour a record's `game` override.

Not drafted, still OPEN: metro station Noord over the bus station (Nieuwe Leeuwarderweg). Also two inferred cuts
with no BGT structure near 4.9399, 52.3991 (ramp 378320520, slot 605321440).

## Tunnel approaches (2026-11-12)

`data/crossings/tunnels.json`: 4 reviewed `road-under` records with BGT tunnel structures: the IJtunnel north
approach, the IJtunnel south at the Prins Hendrikkade, the IJtunnel under the Oosterdok, and the Piet Heintunnel by
the Panamakade. Each keeps the game's inferred cuts and mouths (`game: { cuts: 'keep', portals: 'keep' }`). The
inferred mouths there could not be matched to a portal by eye with confidence, so the records are G2's input for
placing portals, not a runtime change. The other 21 drafts in that area are decks, left for a later pass.
Inferred cuts with no BGT structure (`cut-no-structure` in the draft reports) remain to be checked.

## Round 2 (2026-11-12): the Kimpoweg tram, the runtime extract

The integrator's gate failed `kimpoweg-rails` with the register on: 1 deck span against a pin of 0 (user report #13).
`sloterdijk-carrascoplein`'s metro deck was one BGT cluster 850 m along the Sloterdijk viaduct (52.3845–52.3922), and
its zone took in the Kimpoweg tram at 4.8392, 52.3860–52.3866. The viaduct really does pass over the tram there:
panorama `recording_2025-07-01_09-03-48_02081` shows the tram rails at street level among the viaduct's columns. The
tram lies along the deck's axis, inside its polygon, so the register made a span for it. Two fixes:
- **Data:** the record's decks and zone are cut to the Carrascoplein (52.3870–52.3905); verdict `corrected`.
- **Code:** a record deck makes a span only for a track whose bed is at deck level (see above). This also covers the
  tram terminus on the Carrascoplein (`sloterdijk-rail-bridge`), which runs at grade under the station deck.

`check-rail-street.ts` now builds its after store with `crossings: true`, so every pin holds with the register on.
`check-ground-cells --crossings` builds its own `crossings: true` store.

Bundle: the authored files had grown `own-ground-game` to 3.33 MB. The runtime now bundles only `runtime.json`
(tunnel polygons left out: nothing at runtime reads them; G2's offline bake reads the authored files).
