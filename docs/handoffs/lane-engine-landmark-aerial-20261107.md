## Where the draws come from

- `public/canal-drive/js/signature-landmarks-source.js` (1,269 lines, legacy JS, bundled by `build:canal-signature-landmarks`): one `GLTFLoader` (`:302`, meshopt decoder), one `Object3D` per resident model, one `Mesh` per primitive; the 461 full GLBs have **8 materials / 8 primitives median, 21 max**, no textures, no vertex colours (materials are plain colours normalised to `MeshLambertMaterial` by `landmarks/landmarkShading.ts`). So ~8 draws per model, ~2,200 for 272 resident, of which the frustum keeps ~1,300 in view from the air.
- LOD today: `landmarks/landmarkLod.ts` `chooseLevel` by footprint radius in pixels (`LOD_UPGRADE_RADIUS_PX` 30 / `LOD_DOWNGRADE_RADIUS_PX` 20) picks `lod1` (meshopt-simplified to 15 % of the triangles, same materials, `<id>.lod1.glb`, 404 of 461 models, 24 MB total) or `full`. lod1 keeps the same primitive count, so it saves triangles, not draws.
- Residency: `landmarks/landmarkResidency.ts` (`landmarkReachM` 2,000 m desktop / 1,000 touch, budget 72 / 24, `pickEvictions` furthest-first off-view; the aerial cap `aerialLandmarksMax` 260 / 130). Eviction returns the pand to its extrusion (the coarse shell or far prism; HIDE lists exclude modelled pands from SHL1, so beyond the reach a landmark is its pand prism from the city tiles).
- Shadows: `rendererShared/shadowStandIns.ts` already merges a model's casting meshes into one position-only geometry per shadow side (`_rebuildShadowStandIns` `:1242`), so the shadow pass is ~1 draw per model. The main pass has no equivalent.
- three.js is `^0.185`: `BatchedMesh` (one draw for many geometries sharing one material, per-instance matrix and visibility, `setGeometryAt`/`addInstance`/`setVisibleAt`, `perObjectFrustumCulled`) is available. The models share no material object, but they share a *shader*: Lambert with a flat colour. Colour-to-vertex-colour makes them one material.
