## Why

From the air (user shots in map-recall2-screenshots `engine/2026-11-07-user-shots/`, pitch ~74°, eye ~150 m) the city pops in continuously and a 3-minute pan is 56 frames over 100 ms on desktop. The measurement (`artifacts/perf/quiet/aerial-desktop.json` in worktree `engine-demo-check-20261106`, main cf0f9d02e, headed Chrome on Metal): the baked world is 18 MB gzipped, yet the pan uploads **17.8 GB** to the GPU, 1,780 draws at the worst sample (budget `aerialDrawsMax` 400, report-only), top upload owner `facades:three-building-facades (new geometry)`. The coarse tier (8–13 z14 tiles, 24–37 k buildings, one chunk per tile per kind) is **built at runtime from the cells** every time the ring moves (`worldRing.ts` ringFollow → `threeBuildingsBrowser.ts` `coarse:` groups → worker `buildFeatureChunk(…, 'coarse', …, {shellRoofs: true})`), uploaded, dissolved in over 700 ms, and dropped when the ring leaves. R2-B's #229 hand-back names the churn (~65 MB/s during rides, city-follow stalls) and left step 3 undone.

R2-B already bakes the answer. `npm run world:compile -- --shell` writes `shells/<i_j>.bin` (**SHEL**: the cell's runtime coarse chunk, `scripts/world/shellBake.ts`, the same `buildFeatureChunk` call the worker makes, with shell roofs, the street-appearance catalogue and party-wall context) and `far/<i_j>.bin` (SHL1 prisms, published, 604 cells, 6.8 MB, drawn by `engine/farShells.ts` out to 7 km). SHEL is **unpublished**: 49.6 MB gzipped over 604 cells, median 23.3 KB, max 972 KB; `tiles.json` says `shells: {format: 6, look: 'photo', shel: false, far: true}`. Format: `world/shellMesh.ts encodeShel/decodeShel`, `ShellMesh {positions (metres from shellOrigin), uvs, layers, tints, accents, bids (uint16 building index in the cell), indices}`; positions 0.1 m units (`SHEL_UNIT_M`), uvs `UV_UNITS` 4096. What SHEL lacks against the runtime coarse build (from #229): loaded street fronts (doors on the street side), host wall openings, surveyed envelopes, and the ground lift (`ownGround/chunkBases.ts liftPands`); buildings in the cell's HIDE list (installed models, kits) are left out at bake time.
