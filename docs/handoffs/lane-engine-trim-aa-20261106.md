The delta map says the moving pixels are roof eaves, parapet and cornice lines and the edge-on east elevation: thin geometry under MSAA, not façade texture. Analytic windows did not move it. - **Both rounds measured from the wrong place.** `SWAP_SPOTS.s3-slab` (`scripts/world/abSpots.ts:22`: rider [4.9058, 52.3638], camera [4.9058, 52.3645], z17.6 b-15 p58) looks down from far above; in the r2 strip (`engine/2026-11-06-r2/r2a-s3-desktop-dpr2.jpg`) the slab is a sliver at the lower right.
