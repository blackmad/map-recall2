# Kit lane K-stoep: canal-house stoops with iron railings (opus, 2026-11-10)

**User, 2026-11-10**, on `whats-new/2026-11-10-facade-extras.jpg`: "are we going to get these stairs in our kits?"
The photo shows a canal-belt stoep: a flight of 6–12 stone steps rising along the facade (or straight out) to a
bel-etage door on a landing (bordes) about 1.5–2.5 m up. It has wrought-iron balustrades with posts and newels and a
souterrain door or window under the flight. The game draws two pale slab steps at a door on the street.

## Why this is a kit lane (horizon rule)

- 440 of the 5,958 register buildings in `monument-facts.json` name a stoop.
- 13 hand intents already say `basement: stoop|stoop-and-windows`.
- Jev reads stoop at 0.94.
- That is far past the weighted 50, so the house lanes ship with today's slab steps and record the limit. This lane adds the
  component.

## What exists

- **Canal-house kit:** `buildingRecipe/intent.ts` `BASEMENTS` (`stoop`, `stoop-and-windows`) and `souterrain`.
  `doorReveal.ts` climbs the stoop to the door.
- **Post-war generator:** `buildingTypes/generate.ts` `doorUnit` stoop (#85) has straight steps, rise and tread, and
  sloping handrails. Reuse its maths; do not fork it.
- **Railings:** `fit.ts TIMBER_BALCONY` `rail: iron` grille and the French-window balconette rails. Share one iron-railing
  component (posts, rails, newel, optional baluster pattern) between balconies and stoops.
- `facadeExtras.ts:131` notes that railings must belong to the stoop.

## Deliver

1. **Typed stoop component.** These fields, all optional:
   - `flight: front | side-left | side-right | double`
   - `steps`, `riseM`, `treadM`, `widthM`
   - `landing` (depth, width)
   - `material: stone | brick | painted`
   - `rail: iron | stone | none`, `railStyle: plain | bars | ornate`
   - `under: door | window | closed`

   Every field must be byte-identical when absent. Validation must reject impossible geometry: the flight must fit the
   pavement depth from BGT or the measured stoep zone, and the landing must meet the door sill.
2. **Wiring.** Wire the component into the canal-house recipe path, block-face chunks and HouseSpec `design` (with an H1
   adapter field and provenance). Register `stoop` and Jev `stoop` are the writers for presence. Flight direction and
   step count come from the photo or a hand read.
3. **Generic streamed path.** A pand whose spec says stoop draws a simple stoop through the decorator chain, so the
   belt gets stoops without GLBs. Measure the per-cell triangle cost and keep it within the cell budget.
4. **Measured on photos.** Cover Nassaukade 145–152 (the user's photo row), Herengracht and Keizersgracht examples from
   the register list (front, side and double flights), and one souterrain shop under a stoop.
   - Use `house:preview` compare sheets.
   - Use `house:frames` ride pairs once H10 lands. The likeness bar is the photo: flight direction, step count ±1,
     railing reads as black iron.
5. **Documentation.** Add a kit-gap queue entry closing `stoop`. Document the fields in `docs/house-line.md` and the
   intent JSDoc, and add unit tests for the geometry and validation.
