## Large-recipe roofs inherit 3DBAG noise (user, 2026-10-26)

The user flagged two buildings: Warmoesstraat 157-163 (the car park) has a jagged multi-level roof, and Van Eeghenstraat 97-101 has an odd flat slab over its gables. The large tier draws every roof part except the measured front crown straight from the 3DBAG LoD2.2 planes, so all 116 installed large recipes carry some of this noise.

This is a crude count from each house's `facts.json` `roofsRD`, not checked by eye:
- **Slivers:** 20 installed houses have 10 or more roof pieces under 4 m².
- **Height levels:** 77 have 6 or more separate flat levels. The median is 8 levels and 3 slivers.
- **The two flagged houses:** the Warmoesstraat garage has 11 slivers and 15 levels; Van Eeghen 97 has 6 slivers and 9 levels.

Proposed fix (one kit lane, Opus): add a roof clean-up step in the large tier.
- Merge flat planes within about 0.5 m of each other.
- Drop slivers under about 4 m², or fold them into the neighbouring plane.
- Clamp lone spikes to the row/crown line, as in [[row-coherence]] in the memory notes.
- Keep the measured front crown authoritative, as in [[roofs-from-photo-not-3dbag]] in the memory notes.

The step should be opt-in per intent first. Render before/after gallery cards for the 20 worst houses, then make it the default and re-record fit-golden and large-mesh goldens once they're reviewed.

## Sentry, read 2026-10-26

- **CANAL-RECALL-H** (null `getLayer`, Chrome Mobile)
  - Cause: after "MapLibre lost the WebGL context", a tap reached `inspectBuilding`, which called `map.getLayer` on a null style.
  - Fixed: guarded on `map.style` in `vector-map.js`.
  - Resolve it after the deploy.
- **CANAL-RECALL-7** (graphics failure, Mobile Safari 26.6)
  - Cause: a WebGL context loss on iPhone. The custom layers `shared-frame-main` and `shared-frame-overlay` cannot be restored after the loss, so the map stays broken until a reload.
  - Real fix: re-add the shared-frame custom layers on `webglcontextrestored`, or show a "tap to reload" prompt. iPhone memory pressure is the likely trigger.
  - Temporary fix (2026-10-26): `mapGuard.ts` offers the reload notice 2.5 s after a loss. It also keeps the notice up when a restore comes back without `shared-frame-main`.
  - Instrumentation: a new `webgl-context-restored` Sentry event carries `restoredOk` and `lostForMs`. Both events now also carry `uptimeMs`, `visibility` and `msSinceHidden`, plus a `probe` with zoom, pitch, three.js geometries/textures/programs, last-frame calls/triangles and the style-layer count.
  - Next: read the new events. `msSinceHidden` small means the loss came from backgrounding the app; high `textures`/`uptimeMs` points at memory. Then fix the root cause (re-add the layers on restore, or trim GPU memory on iPhone).
- **CANAL-RECALL-E** (`DIFFICULTY_PRESETS` of undefined, at game.js:7)
