What a baked cell costs on the GPU today (`engine/bakedCoarse.ts install`, ~line 315): per vertex, `position` Float32 ×3 = 12 B (Int16 decimetres ×3 = 6 B only when the cell is outside the lift radius and keeps its `positionUnit`), `uv` Float32 ×2 = 8 B, `layer` u8, `tint` u8 ×4, `accent` u8 ×4, `thin` u8 ×2, `hidden` u8, `swapSlot` u8, plus the index buffer (u16/u32). About 33 B a vertex; 171 MB is in the order of 5 M vertices resident. `tint` and `accent` are constant per building range (`BakedRange`), `uv` is a cell-atlas coordinate that fits a normalised u16, and positions on the decimetre grid fit Int16 (the kept Float32 copy exists only for the ground lift and the coplanar swap near the rider).
