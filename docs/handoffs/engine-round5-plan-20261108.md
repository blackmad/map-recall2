## Diagnosis (code on origin/main b88f3e734; measured nothing yet, so each lane measures first)

**U2/U4 fuzzy ground under crisp buildings.** The own ground is gated on zoom, the buildings are not. `ownGround/gameGround.ts:407` sets `minZoom: 15`; with the overview→city blend on (`overviewTransition/blend.ts`, `BLEND_START_ZOOM 14`, `BLEND_END_ZOOM 15.6`) the ground dissolves in between zoom 14 and 15.6 and below that the flat overview tiles (`engine/overviewLayer.ts`) are what the camera sees. Buildings draw as coarse z14 shells from zoom 14 and the far prisms beyond. The user's aerial poses (eye 100–200 m, pitch 60–75°) sit at zoom ~15.0–15.6, inside the blend band or just under it, so the land is the blurred overview raster while the buildings are full chunks. In U4-Science Park the horizon band is far-ring ground (`farRing.ts`, 50 m grid, 3 m simplify) which is also soft by design. That is the "crisp buildings, fuzzy land". Whether cells were also *pending* (install queue starved by long frames: one install per frame at `gameGround.ts:545`, pending dropped on every ring recompute at `:649`) is the measurement R5-A makes first.

**U2 start.** The ride reveals the world when the game leaves `GameState.LOADING`; `FrameHost.coveredFrameMs` throttles renders while covered (`frameHost.ts:35`, `ownWorldView.ts:354`). Nothing waits for the ground or the buildings near the start pose: `worldSettled()` exists (`ownWorldView.ts:996`, 2026-11-07) but "Not called by the game". The Free Ride starts at an aerial pose, so U2 is also U4.

**U3 roads** (survey of `ownGround/*` 2026-11-08): BGT polygons per class on LOD 0 cells (`roadSurfaces.ts`, `roadCells.ts` classes carriage/foot/cycle/pedestrian/parking/driveway/island/berm/verge/square), OSM bands as fallback and on LOD 1 (`streets.ts`). Layering is height lift in metres (carriage 0.03, cycle 0.04, pavement 0.14; `streets.ts:47-58`) plus `polygonOffset` per material (asphalt 2, cycle 3; `gameGround.ts:321,911`) with no stencil and no polygon booleans. Causes visible in the code: (1) **bridges have no continuity rule**: ground (`h=0`) and deck (`h≥1`) polygons keep their own BGT widths, a deck polygon failing the `deckShare ≥ 25 %` test is skipped and the grey `DECK_TOP` slab shows (`roadSurfaces.ts:500-581`, `decks.ts:33`), neither red mesh crosses a bridge (`cycleThrough.ts:98`, `surveyMarkings.ts:31`), and OSM bands on bridge ways not in `coverWays` draw over the survey at other widths (`groundCell.ts:532`); (2) **two red meshes at junction mouths**, the cycle-crossing red from `surveyMarkings` and the `cycleThrough` ribbon, same layer, same lift, no dedupe, widths from OSM tags not from the BGT fietspad (`groundCell.ts:556-569`); (3) **grey through red**: red sits 1 cm above asphalt, the cycle drape may float without limit or sag 3.2 cm on 24 m chords (`roadSurfaces.ts:69-73`), polygonOffset gap 1; (4) **no sliver or dissolve step**: the BGT partition is drawn as is, kerb lines follow every shared edge including around slivers; (5) **tunnel edges** come from a 1–2 m SDF mask that discards the land (`groundCell.ts:288-307`, `tunnels.ts`). None of the user's three spots is pinned; `roadRegressions.ts ROAD_SPOTS` has 19 others, `scripts/own-ground/road-strip.ts frames` makes orthophoto | before | after strips.

**U6 bare blocks.** `threeBuildingFeatures.ts:139-145`: a streamed building with no `facade` string or no `facadeStyle` is drawn `bare` in its mapped colour or `BARE_WALL_HEX #c9bfae` ("a shed, a landmark part, a building with no style or colour"). The three user buildings are a 2006 apartment block (23 m, 7 storeys), the U-shaped brick complex at Van Hallstraat/Kattensloot with two round towers, and the block behind the Plantage Muidergracht row. `scripts/large-tier/audit.ts` (`npm run audit:large-tier`) already lists every bare building ≥ 150 m² with a reason; nobody has run it since the large tier shipped.

**U1 flat roofs.** A flat roof is one lid in one colour: a measured slot from `roof-facts` (PDOK orthophoto, `roofs/roofSlots.ts`, `MIN_CONFIDENCE 0.35`) else the mapped `roofColour` else `FLAT_ROOF_GREYS[variant]` (`threeBuildingFeatures.ts:151`), shaded by `BAKED_LID_SHADE` (`lightRig.ts:123`, the sun on an up-facing normal, the brightest shade in the rig). Priors are `gravel #9e9281`, `white-membrane #d9d9d3`, `bitumen-dark #56585e`, `bitumen-light #a99b84`; two of the four unmeasured priors are light, and the lid is lit at full sun. `measuredRoof` furniture is parsed (`roofSlots.ts:198-291`, 0.25 m quantum) and stored on the building; whether anything draws it from the air is R5-E's first measurement.

**U5 fan.** The aerial pan is GPU-bound at DPR 2 with MSAA at native frame rate (round-4 pans: `three render main` 500+ ms summed per 36 samples, shadow map inside it). The render governor (`engine/renderGovernor.ts`) steps the pixel ratio to a 0.75× floor on slow frames, not on power. Nothing lowers the frame rate or the shadow map while the camera is detached and panning.

## Engine-lane rules (2026-11-05, unchanged)

- Work only in your worktree on your branch with `/usr/bin/git`. Never `git stash`, never `pkill`; stop your dev server by the PID on your port (`kill $(lsof -t -iTCP:<port> -sTCP:LISTEN)`). Never `npm install` in the shared checkout; `node_modules` is already linked.
- Do not edit `game.js`, `index.html`, `package.json`, lockfiles, generated bundles, `TODO.md`, `HISTORY.md`, `whats-new-notes.json`, `budgets.json`: hand the integrator the text and numbers instead. New tests live in `src/` next to the module.
- Build to a scratch `--outdir` and run through `BUNDLES_DIR`; the Vite dev server full-reloads on any worktree change and silently hangs a harness run. `scripts/build-canal-engine.mjs` has no `--help`: it builds into `public/` when run.
- Browser runs through the lock: `PW_PORT=<port> /tmp/canal-pw.sh perl -e 'alarm 2400; exec @ARGV' -- npx playwright test <spec> --project=desktop --workers=1 --trace off --reporter=line`; timing runs with `CANAL_PW_EXCLUSIVE=1`. Wrap `git push` in `CANAL_PW_EXCLUSIVE=1 /tmp/canal-pw.sh` (the pre-push hook runs a browser smoke test).
- Acceptance is a measured pan or a pinned spec beside the user's shot, not a strip alone. Every fix has a `?x=0` flag and a named regression location. Then stop; the integrator merges.
