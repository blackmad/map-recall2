## The designs, in order

1. **Albert Luthulistraat 6–32 (1930 stair towers)**: 7 pands, five storeys, stair towers
   rising about 3 m over the coping, face `luthuli-6-20` (+ `luthuli-22-32`). Lane #153
   called it the strongest next type. Kit: `tower` exists (per-axis lights, used by
   `transvaal-1923-portiek-tower`); extend it if the tower's plan or crown differs.
2. **Afrikanerplein 25–55 (1931 plain five-storey grid)**: 14 pands, face
   `afrikanerplein-25-55` (one 1989 infill pand to hold). Likely the cheapest type in the
   buurt: a plain grid, measured palette, flat parapet.
3. **Schalk Burgerstraat 45–131 (1931 diaper brick, corner oriels)**: 9 pands, face
   `burger-45-131`. Kit: a diaper-brick pattern on the wall (a second brick tone in a lozenge
   or band pattern; typed, byte-identical when absent) and corner oriels (`bayWindows.oriel`
   exists; a corner variant may be needed).
4. **The 1922 recessed-loggia rows**: Transvaalstraat 131–143 (7), Colensostraat 13–25 and
   14–28 (8), Vaalrivierstraat 15–21 (4): three-storey rows with a recessed upper-storey
   loggia. Kit: loggias exist only in large intents; add a `loggia` on an ordinary
   `FrontIntent` (storey, axes, depth, rail), byte-identical when absent.

For each: contact sheet and 1 m gridded crops in `staging/block-face/transvaalbuurt/5b/`,
measured storeys/axes/windows/frames/brick/parapet/roof, a type JSON with a match block

palette with evidence), a test with two real pands' facts, the kit part it needs, then
auto-place, gallery-size review, holds with reasons, install what passes, before/after
strip-over-model sheets for the two largest faces of each design, and one street screenshot
per installed row (`tests/e2e/demo-shots.spec.ts`, `PW_PORT=4755`, one Playwright run through
`/tmp/canal-pw.sh`).
