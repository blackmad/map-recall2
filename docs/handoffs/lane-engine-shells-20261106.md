## Do, in this order, each behind a flag and measured

1. **Bake.** New stream tag `SHEL` appended to `STREAM_TAGS`, `WORLD_FORMAT_VERSION` 5 → 6 (versions list gains 6; `decodeCell` reads the tag list by version so v5 cells still decode and a v6 cell without SHEL is legal; `cellFormat.test.ts` gains a round-trip and a v5-compat case). Per 1 km cell, in `world:compile` behind `--shell`: run the cell's buildings through `buildFeatureChunk(…, 'coarse', {shellRoofs: true})` exactly as the browser worker does (same look, so the baked shell equals the runtime coarse shell pixel for pixel where both exist), merge to one mesh, and encode: positions as int16 metres × 10 relative to the cell origin (0.1 m), vertex colour RGB8 (wall tint × shade and the roof colour, so no cell texture is needed at distance), a per-vertex `bid` uint16 (index into the cell's building list, for step 3), uint16/uint32 indices. Then a second level `SHEL1` for the far ring: `MeshoptSimplifier.simplify` on the raw arrays (lock border vertices off, target ≤ 8k triangles or ratio 0.25, error 0.02; it is already in `node_modules`), positions at 0.5 m. Report per-stream bytes from `index.json`: target **SHEL ≤ 150 KB gz and SHEL1 ≤ 40 KB gz per cell** (median), total extract growth stated. The merged `COMPILER_VERSION` bumps; the world stays byte-identical with `--shell` off.
2. **Far tier in the frame** (`?farShells=0`, nothing fetched or drawn when off). A `engine/farShells.ts` layer: for every cell inside the far ring (out to `FAR_MAX_M`, following the same ring/keep hysteresis as `farRing.ts`) but outside the resident building horizon, fetch SHEL1 (decode in the world worker, transfer the buffers), one `Mesh` per cell with a vertex-colour lit material that shares the city tone, depth fade and fog of `cityLook` (a `CITY_VCOL` variant or a thin `MeshLambertMaterial` with `onBeforeCompile`; one program, precompiled). Cells fade in with the far ring, are evicted with it (`FAR_KEEP_M`), and never exceed a resident count you state (budget row `farShellCellsMax`). First establish when the far ring is active at all (#224 saw `far.radiusM` 0; the aerial pan must exercise it) and make `aerial-pan.mjs` record far shell cells, bytes and draws. Gate: from the S1 and S4 aerial poses the city continues to the horizon (strip against the user's shots), **draws ≤ 400 at 60° pitch**, city bytes on a Free Ride from the air ≤ 10 MB + the SHEL1 bytes you report.
3. **Baked shells inside the horizon** (`?bakedShells=0`), only after step 2's numbers hold: the resident-ring coarse chunks come from SHEL instead of the runtime `coarse` worker build, keeping the per-building `hidden` attribute (from `bid`) so `refreshDetailOwnership`, the complementary dissolve (`swapSlot`, `?swapComplement`) and `?shellRoofs` semantics are unchanged; the worker no longer builds coarse chunks when the cell has SHEL. This is the upload-churn and stall lever: measure `aerialUploadGB`, `uploadsOver1MBAfterWarmup` and the "city follow" phase before/after. If `swap-zfight` or `swap-gate` move at all, stop and report rather than tune.
