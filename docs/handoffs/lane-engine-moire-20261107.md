The facade tier (`roofTier.ts cellTierSample`, `far = smoothstep(cellTier.x, cellTier.y, rho)`, `cellTier = [16, 30] × pixelRatio` since R1-B's tier-follows-DPR, i.e. [32, 60] at DPR 2) flattens 37 % of the wall to its mean colour and draws the rest as the grid. Histograms, strips `engine/2026-11-06-r3/r3d-*.jpg`.

So: at 16–32 texels per pixel the sampler reads mip level 4–5 (a 256 px cell is 16 or 8 texels across). The slab's window pitch is a few texels at those levels, close to the texel pitch, and the GL box chain keeps 71 % of that contrast per level. One texel per pixel of a near-Nyquist grid is the moiré. The quay houses of S2 (chase at 8–16 texels per pixel, level 3–4, window pitch 5–10 texels there) do not alias and must stay as sharp as they are.
