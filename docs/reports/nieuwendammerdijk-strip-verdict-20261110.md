## Verdict: INSTALL-WITH-FIX for 5 pands, HOLD 1

The type reads right overall. The photo and the model agree on:
- a one-storey red-brown brick front;
- two white-framed casements and a dark door with a fanlight;
- a box dormer on a black tiled roof.

Window sizes match the photo within a few pixels. For example, Nieuwendammerdijk 167049's ground windows are about 1.0–1.1 m wide by 1.25 m high in both. I would not enlarge them.

**Two pands are mirrored the wrong way.**

The photo alternates door and dormer sides pand by pand: right, left, right, left, right, left. The model draws left, left, left, left, right, left.

| Pand | Photo | Model | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0363100012163617 (pand 1) | dormer at about 0.45–0.98 of the front, door at about 0.8, both on the right | both on the left | flip the mirror |
| 0363100012167155 (pand 3) | dormer at about 0.6–1.0, door at about 0.8 (house number beside it), both on the right | both on the left | flip the mirror |

**The dormer surround should be white, not cream stone.**
- Photo: every dormer is a flush white box, with white face, cheeks and fascia, and the frames sit in it.
- Model: the dormer has a cream stone surround.
- Fix: make the dormer body and surround white, about #f0f0ec, flush with the frame, and drop the stone ring.
- The width (about 2.5 m) and placement are fine.

**HOLD 0363100012162650 (pand 6).** Its front differs from the type: a white balustrade (balcony) in front of the dormer, with white pilaster and lintel stonework at roof level. The type cannot express that front.

**Roof.**
- The black tiles match.
- The 3DBAG 6.3 m ridge can stay. The rectified strip foreshortens the receding roof slope, so the photo cannot show the true ridge height, and the dormer top lines up with the photo.
- The photo also shows flat roof lights on the side opposite the dormer (pands 1, 2, 3 and 6). The model draws none. Not blocking; this is a kit gap worth noting.

**Brick.** The red-brown tone matches the sunlit 2022 photo. No change.
