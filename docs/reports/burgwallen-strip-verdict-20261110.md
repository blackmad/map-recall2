# Burgwallen strip review: 23 hand-authored pands on 9 faces, 2026-11-10

Reviewer: Opus strip reviewer. Sheets: `kalverdriehoek-20261110/sheet-{kalver-182-186,kalver-195-199,kalver-204-210,spui-11-13}.jpg`
and `bg-terrein-20261110/sheet-{kloveniersburgwal-90-102,achterburgwal-156-160,-167-169,-175-179,-202-206}.jpg`.
I compared the photo and model side by side at 40 px/m. Brick fixes follow the usual rule: move 60 % of the way from the model to the photo median (the wall gate's
photo value where it is legible), one value per run unless the pands differ.

Two caveats:
- Kalverstraat 195-199 is badly over-exposed (2025-01-20). Brick hex values from that strip are suspect: apply at most half of the pin there.
- The Kloveniersburgwal 90-102 sheet holds only **two** pands (100 and 90).

What remains is gable shape on a few pands, colour pins, some row offsets, and roof faces drawn where the photo shows sky.

## Kalverstraat 182-186

- **186 Sidestep.**
  - The dark painted front, three bays, three rows, shopfront and four-pane windows all match.
  - The top two rows sit about 0.5–0.6 m low. Raise them.
  - The photo's cornice is a heavy white entablature on consoles; the model draws a thin cream band. Deepen it to about 1 m.
    Part of the height in the photo is the soffit seen from below.
- **184 My Brand.**
  - Two wide bays match.
  - The top row is about 0.9 m low, and the windows are shorter than in the photo. Raise the heads.
  - The wall in the photo is neutral black-grey (`#3a383a`); the model's is brown. Use **`#393636`**.
  - Deepen the cornice, as on 186.
- **182 The Amazon, step gable.**
  - The step gable, white bands, white block arches and balcony row all match.
  - The bands and blocks in the photo are **white**; the model's are sand-coloured. Use about `#e8e6e0`.
  - The gable's first step sits about 1.2 m higher in the photo, and the apex about 0.75 m higher. Lift the gable.
  - Brick: the photo is `#564e4b` and the model `#5f433e`. Use **`#5a4a46`**.

## Kalverstraat 195-199 (over-exposed strip)

- **195 Blue Tomato.**
  - Six bays, three rows and the cream fascia shopfront match.
  - The cornice in the photo is deep, on consoles; deepen the model's thin band.
  - Brick: the gate fails on hue, with the photo yellower. Given the exposure, move about halfway to the photo: **`#a58e79`** at the 60 % rule, or about `#9e8470` at half.
- **197.**
  - The white voussoir blocks, cornice with blocks, rows and dark shopfront match.
  - The wall gate fails, but the strip is washed out, so I set no pin.
- **199: gable shape.**
  - In the photo the top is a **flat cornice across the full width**, with a **narrow raised neck**, about 2 m wide and 2.5 m tall, centred on it and capped by a sculpted crown.
  - The model draws a full bell gable with curved shoulders sweeping to the party walls. Make it a narrow neck on a flat cornice.
  - The rows are 0.5 m low. The blue shopfront with its central door matches.

## Kalverstraat 204-210

- **HEMA.** The plaster front, narrow window slits, gable outline and red fascia all match. Install.
- **Cruyff.**
  - The neck in the photo is about **1.4 m** wide and pointed, set in a white sandstone surround. The model's is about 2.6 m and flat-topped. Narrow it.
  - Brick: the photo is `#796b64`. Use **`#7d6960`**.
  - The rows and the window bands match.
- **Pearle.**
  - The neck and crown match.
  - The gable shoulders and the wide gable window sit about 1.1 m higher in the photo; lift them.
  - The model's neck window is a narrow slit, but the photo has a three-light window about 1.6 m wide. Widen it.
- **Dogo.**
  - The paint in the photo is mid grey (`#797776`); the model's is too dark and blue (`#59585e`). Use **`#6c6b6c`**.
  - The top row sits about 0.9 m higher in the photo; raise it.

## Spui 11-13

- **Hampe, step gable.**
  - The black paint, steps, white coping and white ground storey with the door on the right all match.
  - The main gable window in the photo is a **two-light window about 1.3 m wide**; the model's is a narrow slit. Widen it.
  - Add the **small window in the top step**.
- **Provelo.**
  - The black paint, white block arches, rows and door on the right match.
  - The top of the front in the photo is a **white sculpted aedicule dormer** with a pediment and a small balustrade, standing on the cornice, with no slate visible.
    The model draws a slate mansard with a plain cream dormer.
  - At minimum, hide the slate and make the dormer white with a pediment. The sculpture is a kit gap, along with the listed arched base and blind arches.

## Kloveniersburgwal 90 and 100

- **100.**
  - Four bays, the rows, the white bands and blocks, and the door in bay 2 all match.
  - The photo shows a **white entablature on consoles**, with a narrow stepped dormer-gable behind it whose top is about 1.2 m lower than the model's, and **no slate**.
    The model shows a wide slate band and a tall gable. Hide the slate, lower the gable, and add a white entablature.
  - The lane lists this as a limit; it is still the likeness, so fix it.
- **90.**
  - The top row sits about 0.9 m low. The photo has white block arches directly under the cornice; the model leaves a brick frieze strip there.
  - The mansard slate is not visible in the photo, which is fine to leave.
  - The copper-capped dormers are a listed limit.

## What the kit still cannot express

- A narrow raised neck on a flat cornice (Kalverstraat 199), distinct from a bell gable.
- Heavy entablatures on consoles (Kalverstraat 186 and 184, Kloveniersburgwal 100).
- A white sculpted aedicule dormer (Provelo), and a raised white-stone centre with a crest (178647).
- Arched basement doorways (178646, 179784).
