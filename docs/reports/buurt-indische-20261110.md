## 1. What the two buurten are

Pand counts here are the survey's (BAG pands whose units lie in the buurt: MF01 457, MG01 447; the ride-ring report's 571/514 count BAG units).

* **Timorpleinbuurt-Noord** (457 pands, 401 built by 1945, 76 proposed faces, library before this lane: none). A patchwork of
  one design per street: 1902-05 five-storey ornamented rows (Java 1-91, Banka, Deli, Madura 2-76: 55 pands, many designs
  per face), 1912-13 rows, and 1914 social-housing rows. Intake (strip + facts + uses) ran for the 26 faces of 4+ pre-1920
  pands: 267 pands, coverage 1.0 on every face (2019-2025 panoramas). The other 190 pands are single-pand faces (Celebes,
  Timorplein) and the post-1945 infill.
* **Makassarpleinbuurt** (447 pands, 287 built by 1945, 101 faces, 19 covered, library `indische-buurt`: 3 types for the
  1924-27 blocks). 34 pands installed earlier (Molukken-, Gorontalo-, Niasstraat). Strips of the 21 faces with intake were
  re-pulled (the 2026-10-17 strips were never archived).
