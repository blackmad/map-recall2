## Reading

- The tidy is mild by design: most "levels" in 3DBAG are real tiers more than 0.5 m apart, and most slivers are tilted facets
  whose topology cannot be dissolved without rebuilding the roof mesh. It removes sub-0.5 m level noise and clamps lone spikes
  (224 -> 124 across the 163 houses), and leaves the front planes untouched.
- Van Eeghenstraat 97 (the flat slab over the gables) has one flat level and no spike: the slab is real 3DBAG geometry, so the
  tidy cannot fix it. It needs the roof drawn from the measured gables (pitched roof behind the step gables), which the large kit
  cannot yet express.
- The Warmoesstraat garage keeps 9 levels: its stepped car-park decks are genuine.
