`leliegracht-20261110/sheet-herengracht-84-148-r1.png`, both from 10:02. I compared the photo and model side by side, used the overlay panels,

## Keizersgracht

**258A (169386)**
- What matches: three bays, four rows, the door at the left of the ground storey, and the brick (`#736450` against `#706150`).
- The photo's defining feature is **wide white eared frames on every window**, about 0.2 m of white each side, with ears at the heads. The model draws thin frames.
  The lane lists this as a limit. On this front it is the likeness, so widen the frames, or hold the pand if the kit cannot.
- The iron balconettes on the bel-etage are listed as a limit and are acceptable.

**256 (169390), neck gable: hold**
- The overlay shows the gable is in the wrong place.
  - The photo's shoulder line, where the claw pieces sit, is about **14 m** up. The model's is about 15.6 m (eaves 15.9 m).
  - The model's neck top sits about 1.6 m above the photo's brick neck.
- The ground storey matches. The upper storeys are stretched to reach the high gable:
  - top-storey heads are about **1.5 m** high;
  - second-storey heads about **1 m** high;
  - third-storey heads about **0.5 m** high.
- **Fix:**
  - lower the shoulder and eaves to about 14 m;
  - put the storey heads on the photo lines;
  - lower the neck by about 1.6 m.
- The **neck window** in the photo is about 1.3 m wide and 1.6 m tall, sitting just above the shoulders. The model's is about 0.9 m and placed high in the neck. Make it larger and lower.
- The neck width, the door on the right with its white surround, three bays, and the pinkish-tan brick all match.
- The ornate crown and the claw scrolls are kit limits.
- Re-sheet after the fix.

**254 (169444)**
- **Wall.** The photo is a smooth cream-yellow **painted render**, `#ebdbad`. The model is a pale brick texture, `#dcd3bc`, and the gate fails. Use **`#e5d8b3`**, without the brick texture if the kit allows.
- **Panes.** The photo windows are small-paned and **three lights wide**: about 12-light casements on the top storey, and three-wide sashes below. The model draws two-wide lights.
- **Top.** In the photo the top is a straight cornice with a raised **semicircular crest** about 3.5 m wide and about 1.5 m tall, holding a cartouche. The model draws it as a bell gable.
  The silhouettes are close, so this is acceptable as an approximation. A cornice with a crest is a kit gap.
- The door at the left with its white surround, three bays, and the rows all match.

## Herengracht

**96B (167714)**
- **Door bay.** In the photo the door is in the **third bay** of four, with a white pilastered surround, and bay 4 is a tall window. The
  model puts the door in bay 4. Move it to bay 3. This is likely the cause of the "ground bays 1 vs 4" gate failure.
- **Top.** In the photo, a **triangular pediment about 4.5 m wide, over the centre two bays**, sits on the cornice, with a **dormer on each side**. The model has a single
  small pedimented dormer in the centre, and the red roof shows. Make it a pediment on the cornice with two flanking dormers, or list the
  pediment as a kit gap if the kit has no cornice pediment.
- **Frames.** The photo has wide white eared surrounds on all windows, and white pilasters at the ground storey. Widen the model's frames, as on 258A.
- The wall reads as grey paint, `#a7a08a`. The model's `#a19474` is within the gate but warmer. This is optional.
- The cornice consoles and the stoop railing are listed limits.

**94A (175699)**
- Three bays, the door on the right, the three attic lights and the rows all match.
- The wall gate fails (`#d7c19d` against `#a08f75`), but the front is **in full sun** in this strip. Corrected for the sun, the model tone is right, so no pin.
- The crest ornament on the cornice and the stoop railing are limits.

**92 (175700)**
- Three bays, the door on the left, the three attic lights, the crest ornament (drawn as the small dome) and the brick (`#93816e` against `#897869`) all match.

## What the kit still cannot express

- Wide white eared window surrounds. They define the look of 258A and 96B.
- A pediment on the cornice over several bays (96B), and a straight cornice with a raised semicircular crest (254).
- Neck-gable crowns and claw scrolls (256).
- Smooth painted render without brick texture (254), if the kit has no render material.
