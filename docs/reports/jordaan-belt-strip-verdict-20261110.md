## The pattern behind most holds

Many of these canal houses have a **raised bel-etage over a souterrain**, or **two or three tall storeys** where the model
has drawn three or four standard ones. The kit's storey grid is placed from the ground up with standard storey heights. On these fronts that puts the model's
window rows between the photo's, which is plainly visible in the overlay panels. A front whose rows miss the photo by 0.6 m or more is a hold, not
"near enough". The fix is a per-pand storey grid taken from the photo, plus a basement storey where the photo shows one.
