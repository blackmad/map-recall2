# Ground G1: targeted fixes, measured (2026-11-12, issue #376)

Lane G1 of the Ground v2 plan (`docs/handoffs/ground-v2-plan-20261110.md`), after G0's audit (`docs/reports/ground-audit-20261110.md`). Every fix sits behind a URL flag, default on, listed in `src/canalRecall/ownGround/groundFixes.ts` (page → worker init → `GroundStore` option → `CellInput`). The A/B is `ground:audit --spots` at G0's nine named places (150 m windows, 0.5 m raster), plus the city run. The ranking by G0's area-per-tile score was ruled wrong, so it is not used here.

## Tools added
- `audit-ground.ts --pairs`: poke and z-fight per layer pair (which two layers fight). `--opts '{"floatBound":false}'` turns a fix off for the whole run. `--map a=b` reads a staged extract.
- `probe-portal-sanity.ts`: closed mouths and unsupported mouths per G0 cell, with portal sanity on and off.
- `probe-road-overlap.ts`: roads-v1 against the OSM street ribbons, from traced triangle sources.
- `probe-g1-cost.ts <fix>`: triangles and build time per cell with a fix on and off.
- `check-ground-spots.ts`: the G1 pins (below).
- `clip-open-ground.ts`: open-ground-v1 minus the roads-v1 ground polygons, written to `staging/open-ground-v2`.
- `g1-strip.ts`: orthophoto beside the before and after frames.
- `tests/e2e/ground-g1.spec.ts`: the Overschiestraat underpass ride, and opt-in frames (`G1_FRAMES=1`, `G1_BEFORE=1`).

## Items

**A. Open ground against the roads (`?openGroundClip=0`): measured null.** The plan-area overlap of open-ground-v1 with the roads-v1 ground polygons is 1,105 m² across the city (143,856 areas, 2,961 touched) and 43 m² in the six named cells. The A/B with the clipped extract staged gives +9 m². Open ground's 16.2 % share in G0's A/B comes from three other things:
- z-fight with the land left under its edges (land<garden 1,724 m², land<shrub 397 m²);
- its own thin slivers (+4.1k m²);
- float (+3.6k m²).

D takes most of the float. Do not publish open-ground-v2; the store reads it only when it is published.

**B. roads-v1 (`?roadStrips=0`).** I measured both suspects:
- OSM ribbons over the survey: 140 m² overlap in cell −4_−3, against 289,579 m² of survey.
- drapeAdaptive's min-area gate: −571 m² once the gate holds only for compact triangles (longest edge ≤ 2 data steps), mostly poke (7,604 → 6,701). Cost: +3.1 % triangles.

Neither suspect is roads-v1's 31 % stake. That is earcut's thin slivers in the survey's strips: with roads off, thin falls from 25.3k to 9.0k m². Fixing it means triangulation quality (CDT with Steiner points), which goes to G2.

**C. Portal sanity (`?portalSanity=0`, `tunnels.ts PORTAL_SANITY`).**
- A closed mouth needs a tunnel-tagged way within 25 m ahead and both sides bounded (a wall, merged gap or median within 2 stations).
- Motor ramps need the both-sides test too.
- Parallel tunnel runs within 10 m become one slot.
- A slot's portal level is the lower median of its samples, not the minimum.

Results in cells 2_3, −3_4, −4_−4, −4_−3 and −5_−3:
- cuts 24 → 20, slots 3 → 2, closed mouths 14 → 7;
- mouths with no tunnel vertex within 25 m: 4 → 2. The two left are the Coentunnel's own mouths, with OSM's tunnel vertex 26 m ahead.
- Overschiestraat is one slot reaching 8.6 m each side.

At the named places: −472 m². check:tunnels passes.

**D. Float bound (`?floatBound=0`).**
- Every natural layer drapes on the 5 m land grid's surface, which floats over the relief in each hollow narrower than a quad. `drape.ts floatBoundGrid` splits such quads to 2.5 m and fans their neighbours watertight. It is a pure function of position, and byte for byte the old grid where nothing splits.
- drapeBisect's new float test (`parks.ts DrapeBound`, 6 cm over the land) splits fills that bridge a hollow.

Results:
- Land alone: 67,196 → 59,399 m². With the fill bound: 53,766 m².
- Float 26,462 → 13,409 m².
- Cost: +7–11 % triangles per cell (−4_−3 +8.4 %, −5_1 +10.9 %); build time within noise.
- A 4 cm fill bound gave 53,444 m² at +11–14 % triangles.

Full detail (desktop, LOD 0) only.

**E. Caps under decks: not built; measured instead.**
- Along Piet Kranenbergpad under the rail and ring decks, the relief runs smoothly from 0.3 to 0.9 m: there is no saddle on the path.
- The deck places' remaining defects:
  - Road layers above a deck top: Radarweg asphalt/paving/cycle<deckTop 957 m², Kranenbergpad 729 m². The covered deck top is sunk under the survey's ground polygons by design (`COVERED_TOP_SINK_M`), so this is the audit's precedence, not a visible fault.
  - z-fight of the parks-v1 lawn with the OSM park fill (lawn<park 1,246 m² at Kranenbergpad).
- A cap that flattens the relief under a dry deck also drops the road on that deck, unless dry way decks are on. The integrator turned those off on 2026-11-02.
- G3's crossing records (`origin/lane/ground-g3-crossings-20261110`) do not exist on the remote.

E belongs with G2's deck constraints.

**F. Underpass roads and pins.**
- Pins: `check-ground-spots.ts`:
  - each named place's shard area ≤ G1 + 5 %;
  - Kranenbergpad float ≤ 400 m² and poke > 5 cm ≤ 900 m²;
  - Overschiestraat one slot ≥ 6 m each side;
  - no unsupported mouths in 2_3 and −4_−4.
- The underpass e2e: `tests/e2e/ground-g1.spec.ts`, which rides through Overschiestraat on the own engine.
- Keeping BGT h < 0 road polygons under the cuts needs roads-v1 rebuilt from the raw BGT wegdeel. The cache is gone from every worktree, so this needs a fetch and an integrator publish: left open.
