# Rapenburg 97 and 99: strip review, 2026-11-10

Reviewer: Opus strip reviewer. Sheet: the current staging copy,
`staging/block-face/rapenburg-89-103-r1/review/strip-sheet.png` (09:26), not the archived copy.
The photo strip and the model were compared side by side on a 0.5 m grid at 40 px/m. Hex values are medians of
pier boxes between windows; the strip has a slight green-grey cast.

## Verdicts

| Pand | BAG | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Rapenburg 97 (cornice house, office) | 181605 | **install-with-fix** |
| Rapenburg 99 (bell gable, shop + dwelling) | 170674 | **install-with-fix** (if the ground storey and the gable neck cannot be fixed, hold) |

Both are recognisable: the bay counts, storey count, white ground storey on 97, cornice band, and bell-gable
silhouette with its single gable window are all there. Each still has errors that read clearly
against the photo. The lane already knows about all four of its flaws, and I confirm each one. The measurements are below, along with
the flaws I found beyond them.

## Rapenburg 97

**Confirmed: the second-storey heads are short.** The real error is larger than the lane's figure. In the photo the second-storey
windows run about 2.1 m from sill to head. The model's run about 1.6 m, with the **head about 0.9 m too low and the sill about 0.45 m
too low**. The first storey is also short: the photo is about 2.1 m, the model about 1.75 m, and the **head is about 0.45 m low** while
the sill matches. Raise both heads so that all three upper rows line up with the photo heads.
The top-storey windows are about 1.5 m in both, with the model 0.2 m low at the head, which is acceptable.

**Confirmed: the panes.** The second and first storeys in the photo are tall two-light sashes, with one vertical bar and no
cross bar. The model draws them as four-pane cross windows. The **top storey is correct as a four-pane cross window**,
so change only the lower two rows.

**Found by the reviewer:**
- **Cornice depth.** The photo's white cornice and frieze is about 2.2 m deep, from the top moulding to the bottom
  fascia. The model's cream band is about 1.6 m, and its lower edge sits about 0.55 m above the photo's. Extend the
  frieze down by about 0.5 m. Below it there is a brick strip of about 0.9 m before the top-storey heads; that part already matches.
  The small attic vent in the frieze and the central finial on the crown are not modelled. The lane's sheet lists the frieze as a limit, so this is acceptable.
- **Sills.** The model's white sills project wide past the frames. In the photo the sills are flush, and the
  window frames are thick white surrounds with no projecting ledge. Reduce the projection if the kit allows it.
- **Brick.** The photo reads `#3f392c` and the model `#56473b`. Move 60 % of the way to the photo: **`#483f32`**.
- **Ground storey.** The white storey, the three openings (left and right narrow, centre wide), and the transom
  or mezzanine row match closely. Keep them.

## Rapenburg 99

**Confirmed: the gable is wide at the shoulders, and the fault is at the neck.**
- The model's neck is a straight-sided block about 2.8 m wide with a flat top and a small point. In the photo the
  neck is about 2.1 m wide, and on top of it sits a **round-headed crown cap about 1.45 m wide**: the sandstone
  ornament, which the sheet lists as a limit.
- The photo's bell flares from about 1.5 m below the crown. The model's flares from about 3.5 m below it. Lower down, at gable-window
  height, the photo is wider than the model.
- The result is a model gable that is too wide and boxy at the top and too narrow in the lower bell.
- Fix: narrow the neck to about 2.1 m, start the bell curve about 2 m higher, and replace the flat top with a rounded cap.
  If the kit has no round cap, use the closest rounded crown and list the gap.

**Confirmed, and wider than the lane listed: the ground storey.**
- The photo's ground storey is **white across the full width**. It has a white beam or cornice about 1 m high on top, then a
  **continuous row of transom lights across the whole front** (five lights, about 1 m tall). Below that row:
  - **two doors side by side at the left**, about 0.75 m and 0.8 m wide, both black;
  - a multi-pane shop window to the right, about 2.5 m wide, in three or four white-mullioned lights.
- The model has brick down to the ground at the left, with a narrow door in it. White starts only over the right three-quarters.
  It has two large single-pane plates and a door in the middle.
- Fix:
  - make the white storey full width;
  - move the middle door left to sit next to the first door, using a door pair;
  - add the transom row;
  - divide the shop glazing with mullions.
- Without these fixes the ground storey does not read as this house, so the pand is a hold.

**Found by the reviewer:**
- **Gable window.** The photo's window is about 1.0 m by 1.0 m, with its sill just above the foot of the gable. The model's is about
  0.7 m wide and set about 0.75 m higher, with a vent above it that the photo does not show. Widen the window to about 1.0 m,
  drop it to the photo sill, and remove the vent.
- **Upper storeys.** Window width (1.1 m), count and spacing match. The second-storey heads are about 0.35 m low and the
  first-storey heads about 0.25 m low. Raise them if the 97 fix touches the same storey grid; otherwise this is acceptable.
  The pane layout (small upper lights over a two-light lower sash) is acceptable at this resolution.
- **Brick.** The photo reads `#3a2f22` and the model `#564336`. The gate passed, but the difference is plainly visible. 60 %: **`#45372a`**.
  These are per-pand pins, because 97 and 99 are separate lane pins with different brick.
- **Door side.** The doors are on the left in both the photo and the model, so there is nothing to mirror.

## What the kit still cannot express here

- Rapenburg 99: the round sandstone crown cap and the claw scrolls at the neck shoulders of the bell gable.
- Rapenburg 97: the vent in the cornice frieze and the central finial.
- A transom row that runs continuously over a pair of doors and a shopfront, if the shopfront component cannot span the doors.
