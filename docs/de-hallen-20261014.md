# De Hallen and the Kinkerstraat halls as large recipes (2026-10-14)

Plan Part 2 step 8. Branch `lane/de-hallen-20261014`. Three pands were measured from archived Gemeente Amsterdam
panoramas (CC BY 4.0) and compiled as `kind: large` recipes:

| house | pand | built | tris | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `de-hallen-236693` | 0363100012236693 | 1902 tram depot (De Hallen) | 10,662 | **staged, not installed** |
| `kinkerstraat-32-137280` | 0363100012137280 | 1999, Kinkerstraat 32-54 | 8,708 | installed (`--allow-inferred`) |
| `kinkerstraat-89-092013` | 0363100012092013 | 1987, Kinkerstraat 89-105 | 3,205 | installed (`--allow-inferred`) |

The brief's premise was wrong in two ways:

- The tram depot is pand 236693, not 092013. Pand 092013 is the 1987 infill across the Kinkerstraat, and 137280 is
  the 1999 block about 400 m east.
- Pand 236693 is already drawn by the bespoke signature landmark `de-hallen` (`signature-landmarks.json`,
  POI card).

Installing the recipe would place a second model on the same footprint. Retiring the landmark touches integrator
hotspots: the generated landmark data, `manualCatalogue.json` and the bundles. The De Hallen recipe is therefore
staged only. The entry is in `artifacts/building-recipes/de-hallen-236693/catalogue-entry.json`, and
`install-large.ts --install` with the notes below installs it once the integrator decides.

Colours are measured medians from the crops. Window shapes are width × height of the bay, with the sill height,

## Tools and kit added on this branch

**Crop tools (commit 538c511e)**

- `all-walls.ts` gains new options, with the logic in typed `src/canalRecall/buildingRecipe/panoPick.ts` (tested):
  - `--date`, `--exclude-date` and `--exclude-pano`: choose panoramas other than the 2024-12-11 run.
  - `--segment=<m>`: cut a long front into equal pieces, each with its own best panorama.
  - `--walls`, `--suffix` and `--min-dist`: re-cut chosen walls and merge the rows into `walls.json`.
- `likeness`:
  - Judges a segment mosaic per crop.
  - The close-camera abstain rule now uses the summed span of pieces shot from under 8 m (`closeCameraSpan`).
  - `panelCoverageStraight` drops pieces more than 45° oblique when the straight pieces cover the panel.

**Large kit (commit 8210611a and this branch)**

- **`hallDoors`:** glazed hall portals on chosen ground bays (round, segmental or flat head, lights × rows, height).
  Arched portals get a 0.12 sill so the arch does not z-fight the plinth.
- **`glazedStoreys`:** upper storeys glazed like a shop ground.
- **`groundClamp`:** clamps sunken 3DBAG faces.
- Every addition is validated and tested, and is byte-identical when absent: `large-mesh-hashes` reports all other
  meshes identical.

**Sheets:** `scripts/building-recipes/index-row-sheet.ts` makes the four-cell index rows.

The brief assumed an "arched hall". The photos show something else:

- The arches are only the two glazed gateways.
- The long side is a pale concrete front with pilasters.
- The gables have flat-headed glazed portals.

So the kit got `hallDoors` rather than an `arched-hall` system.

## Verdict at gallery size

**De Hallen.** At street level from the Bellamyplein, the recipe reads correctly: dark brick gables with
wide glazed portals, the purple-grey corner block, and the gateway arches on the Tollensstraat. That is closer to
the photo than the landmark's uniform blue window strips. From above, the landmark's striped roofs (red and blue)
carry the place better, because the recipe has plain slate saw-tooth roofs with no glass ridge lights. The
Tollensstraat concrete hall is right in colour and rhythm but flat: it has no fins, no scalloped lintel band and no
brick attic.

**Recommendation:** keep the landmark until the kit can say ridge lights and the stepped stone-coped gables. Then
retire the landmark and install the recipe in the same integrator change.

**Kinkerstraat 32-54.** Clearly better than the city box it replaces: orange and dark masses, the shop glazing and
the mezzanine band. It still misses the red awnings and the set-back dormered attic.

**Kinkerstraat 89-105.** A fair rendered block with ribbon windows. It misses the cantilevered balconies and the
yellow-tiled stair towers that define it in the photo.

## What the kit still cannot say

**De Hallen**

- A brick attic band over a concrete hall front.
- A scalloped lintel band and deep concrete fins.
- Stepped gable profiles with stone copings, and the gable oculus.
- Wide rusticated stone arch rings around the gateways.
- Round-arched upper windows in stone surrounds.
- The GEMEENTETRAM lettering.
- Glass ridge lights on saw-tooth hall roofs.
- Eared or stone-dressed window surrounds on brick: the photo mask classes them as trim.
- Per-storey wall colour in the measurement: a stone ground under brick averages into one grey.

**Kinkerstraat 32-54**

- Red shop awnings.
- A dormered set-back attic.
- Mezzanine glazing on one side of a `wallSplit` only.
- A recessed glazed stair slot.

**Kinkerstraat 89-105**

- Cantilevered balconies on chosen bays.
- Yellow-tiled stair towers.
- A blue tiled shop ground.
