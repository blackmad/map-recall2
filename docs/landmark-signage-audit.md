# Landmark lettering audit — 2026-10-04

Scope: one bounded source/code audit of literal `sign`, custom `text` and `glyph` calls in `scripts/landmarks`, including calls in `build-manual-landmarks.ts` missing from the initial inventory. `Math.sign` and helper declarations are excluded. This document does not change builders or accept rendered signage. No gallery/game comparison was performed in this audit.

Policy: architectural lettering requires a reference for its exact words, location and wall plane. A POI name or operator website alone is not evidence of a facade inscription. Keep genuine architectural inscriptions and defining real shop/venue signs. Remove invented identification labels; uncertain real signs need a targeted photograph rather than speculative deletion. Every retained sign still requires actual panel fit and native-scale legibility review.

## Priorities

The four strongest unsupported-label candidates are `HART`, `VAN GOGH`, `HE HUA TEMPLE`, and `GASHOUDER`. Their cached records do not document the modeled inscriptions, and the calls place abbreviated/translated names directly on modeled architecture. **Remove recommendation means remove this unsupported authored label; it does not assert that no real sign exists anywhere on the building.** Root should inspect a current principal-facade reference before applying an uncertain removal. The He Hua plaque must preserve its actual lettering/script if recreated; English POI translation is not a replacement inscription.

Second priority: `DE PIRAMIDES`, `REMBRANDT`, `MONDRIAAN`, `AMSTA DE POORT`, and the prominent `AMSTERDAM CENTRAAL` central-facade name. These lack exact sign-specific provenance in the examined cached notes. Some may represent genuine signs; resolve with a cropped principal-facade photograph before changing them.

Heineken's two real `HEINEKEN BROUWERIJ` signs are explicitly preserved. Moco/Van Loon invented labels and Brakke Grond identification text were already removed by the coordinator; Boom Chicago's real sign was already fitted. Those edits were not repeated here.

## Concrete implementation risks

The shared `sign` helper creates glyph boxes in +Z and uses seven-row letter patterns. Its optional `maxWidth` currently appears at the corrected Boom Chicago call; other panel-backed signs are generally unbounded. Approximate letter height is seven times pixel size, including box extent. Fit must use the actual glyph advances, spaces and a margin, not `text.length` times a fixed width. The custom Rialto helper has a separate five-row glyph system, so shared sizing assumptions do not transfer automatically.

Known code-only concerns to reproduce in render review: Frascati vertical characters overlap by their nominal extents; Madame Tussauds letters exceed the modeled fascia height; the slanted OLVG East entrance and several projecting signs may disagree with default +Z letter orientation. These are potential fit defects, not visual acceptance results.

Missing sign-specific provenance is not proof a real sign is absent. The audit intentionally leaves most source-backed venue/storefront intentions unresolved until an exact reference crop and native-scale render are compared. Cached BAG/OSM names, survey heights, successful generation and map labels do not close that gap.

No builder, asset, queue, shared bundle, git index or commit was changed by this audit worker. Remaining acceptance belongs to the coordinator: compare references/renders, apply justified removals/fits, regenerate affected GLBs/fingerprints and runtime bundles, inspect gallery/live scenes, and preserve any failed evidence.
