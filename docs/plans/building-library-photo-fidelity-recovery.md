Requested 2026-10-02 after the user reviewed the scale gallery. This supersedes the quick-batch expansion priority in the Sol takeover plan. Continue in `.worktrees/sol-building-library` on `agent/sol-building-library`. No commits, deployments or production replacement.

## Problem and target

The gallery demonstrates throughput, but too many drafts fail the intended reasonable-fidelity bar. Generated assets are not completed reconstructions. Even some photo-edited recipes preserve conspicuously wrong BAG upper geometry. Many untouched starters have generic brown walls, uniform sash windows and no actual entrance. Increasing the count without fixing these shared failures is not the next milestone.

The target is a short photo-to-recipe pass that produces a recognizable building: correct dominant roof/front form, plausible height and floor count, correct bay/group arrangement, a deliberate ground-floor/entrance layout, approximate visible colours, and defining trim. Ordinary buildings should take a few stock choices; distinctive buildings should draw from reusable packs. Do not pursue tiny measurements or bespoke perfection.

## 0. Check photo extraction and owner coverage before changing geometry

Bloemgracht29 (owner0363100012168747) is a required coverage regression. The user points out that the photo may show only about two thirds of the BAG owner, or the extraction may be wrong. Do not assume that the label full means the entire building is visible. Compare crop-plane/frontage extent, BAG street envelope, roof alignment and visible edges before applying roof/height or repeating a window layout across the whole owner.

If extraction is wrong, correct the selected facade plane/crop or select better dated evidence. If the owner contains multiple fronts and the photo covers only one part, assign that evidence to an explicit frontage interval/part; retrieve other cached views where available and keep remaining appearance uncertain. Do not stretch a partial image into a whole-owner recipe or lower the whole owner solely from an incomplete crop. Report coverage/identity separately from inferred appearance height. A suspicious crop is a targeted evidence task, not permission to invent the unseen third.

## 1. Replace unreliable BAG appearance roofs and heights

Implement an explicit inferred appearance roof contract. BAG `sourceShell`, owner ID, footprint, placement and original height evidence remain immutable audit inputs. A photo-selected roof may replace displayed upper geometry and may be lower than the BAG maximum. Do not silently rewrite measured data or force preservation of a peak that is precisely the defect being corrected.

Shared roof choices: flat/parapet, gable, hip, mansard and roof behind a decorative single/compound gable. Frontage/gable form and roof shape are separate choices, but the resulting roof must not seal openings or project above the chosen front silhouette accidentally. Support a central/offset dormer and level cornice. Compound fronts may need separate roof parts and independent heights, rather than one stretched template.

For a lower appearance height, retain the BAG height, record the inferred eaves/peak and dated-photo provenance separately, and state which surfaces are replaced. Export checks must distinguish bounded inferred appearance geometry from native peak preservation. Verify owner/footprint/placement preservation; do not falsely claim native roof preservation for a replacement.

Height heuristics are suggestions until the photo pass selects them:

- Ground height plus the observed upper floor count determines the body. Roof triangles are not additional residential floors.
- A flat/parapet choice proposes a peak close to eaves, not the original BAG peak.
- For pitched roofs, use a width-based plausible rise; flag a BAG rise above roughly one ordinary floor when the photo does not show an attic/roof of that size.
- Flag large blank space between the last upper windows and the cornice/profile, especially when taller than an ordinary floor. Offer a lower eaves/peak or an explicit attic, not another generic window row.
- Ignore isolated tall spikes/attachments as body-height evidence. Cropped/occluded roofs remain uncertain rather than automatically corrected.

First proof set: Boomstraat37, Bloemstraat142/154/121, Boomstraat86/88, Derde Egelantiersdwarsstraat8. Inspect front and quarter views against crops before claiming success. Then add the remaining correction cases and apply the same generator to other clear examples.

## 3. Cornices, bands and colours are part of the basic pass

Expose existing stepped/flat cornices as normal recipe choices, with separate roof/eaves and shop-level datums. Add dentils/brackets only as a shared optional pack. Curved/compound gables should use the existing coping path. Avoid opaque trim crossing apertures.

Use pale ground-storey finish bands and horizontal masonry bands where visible. Include obvious ground pillars and timber subdivision. These should be reusable patterns, not per-owner geometry branches.

Select approximate wall, ground, frame, roof and trim colours from dated crops. Do not leave the brown starter colour on a charcoal/gray facade. Colour is approximate under lighting; avoid treating a shadow as a material change. Untouched starters must remain visibly unreviewed.

## 4. Tighten the fast photo-pass workflow

Before calling a recipe a likeness draft, deliberately review: upper count, ground layout/door, front form, roof/height, window grouping/divisions, dominant colours, and defining cornice/trim. An edit to colour alone does not establish that the other choices were reviewed. Existing `photo-pass-draft` means layout edited, not fidelity accepted; keep that explicit.

Show stages clearly in the gallery: starter, layout edited/fidelity pending, correction needed/evidence held, and likeness reviewed only after a real photo/view pass. Provide filters. Do not hide known defects to improve the apparent totals. Add flags for missing entrances and suspicious upper volumes, with explicit exception controls. POI priority remains useful, but obvious warehouse/ornamental/compound forms also justify a shared detail pack.

Author a small representative correction set with the new roof/joinery/trim choices before resuming bulk photo passes. The test is recognizable results and a repeatable short recipe process, not the number of GLBs.
