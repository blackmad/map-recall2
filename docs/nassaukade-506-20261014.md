**Ground floors** are residential (BAG woonfunctie), except the corner bar at 151-152. Each has its door on the left.

## New kit components

Each one is typed in `intent.ts`, laid out in `fit.ts` and built in `oudWest.ts` or the library. With the key absent, the
output is byte-identical to before.

- **`bands: 'painted'`**, with `bandHeights {sill, transom, head}` in **metres** (defaults 0.14, 0.10 and 0.17), `bandLines`
  and `bandStoreys`. These are white stone-surface bands across the whole front, cut at a multi-bay erker:
  - the sill band sits with its top on the storey's lowest sill;
  - the transom band is centred at 0.78 of the window height;
  - the head band stands on the window heads.

  The key names are agreed with the Kinkerstraat lane: `bands: "painted"`, `bandHeights: {sill, head}` in metres, and
  `wallMaterial: "painted-brick"`.
- **`windowSurround: 'eared-keystone'`** is built as separate dressing slabs:
  - a head slab 0.10 m high and 0.08 m deep, running 0.12 m past each jamb;
  - an ear block (0.13 x 0.25 m) at each end;
  - `round(w / 0.4) - 1` keystone blocks.
- **`windowSills: 'consoles'`** is a sill slab (0.08 m high, 0.10 m proud, running 0.12 m past the jambs) on two consoles
  (0.10 x 0.16 m) under the jambs. Balcony bays are skipped.
- **`balconies.style: 'timber-bracketed'`** takes `rail: 'turned' | 'iron'`, `styleStoreys` and `storeyBays`.
  - **Platform:** 0.14 m thick, 0.75 m deep, its top at the storey floor, on 4-point profile brackets every 1.1 m.
  - **Guard:** 1.0 m high, with base and hand rails.
  - **Turned rail:** square-plan balusters every 0.16 m, each a shaft and a bulb, with the back and contact faces culled
    (12 triangles each).
  - **Iron rail:** bars every 0.14 m, in the door colour.
  - **`styleStoreys`** mixes timber platforms and library balconettes on one front.
  - **`storeyBays`** sets the bays per storey, for example s1 full width under a centre s2.
  - A 2 m turned balcony costs under 330 triangles.
- **`bayWindows.finish: 'painted'`** turns the erker's wall panels and cap to the trim colour and surface: the white timber
  erker.
- **`wallMaterial: 'painted-brick'`** gives the wall materials `tintSource: 'measured'`. Chunk buckets are keyed
  `<hex>_brick_measured` and the extra is exported, so the game skips the 0.3-0.6 lightness clamp.
- **The BAG erker chord** (`blockFace/bayOutline.ts`, run as `intake --bay-outline=<pand6>`) fixes 146. BAG draws that pand
  from above with its erker, which gave a 1.61 m "front".
  - The chord walks the ring past the bay's sides to the facade edges.
  - It clips the footprints and roofs and rebuilds the front facts, giving 6.17 m.
  - The uncorrected facts are kept in `facts-bay-outline.json`.
- **`mirrorFront`** now also mirrors `balconies.storeyBays`.

Window naming follows the canal-windows lane (`t-window`, `windowHeights`, `windowHead`, `windowSurround: 'sill'`).

### After the merge with main (2026-10-14)

The lane was merged onto main with the canal-windows, Kinkerstraat and large-tier work. The conflicts in `fit.ts`,
`fitLarge.ts` and `intent.ts` were resolved as the union of both sides; every committed face chunk, all 153 fit-golden
recipes and all 112 large meshes stayed byte-identical. The face then changed as follows:

- **T-windows:** `windows: 't-window'` on both library types and on 151-152 and 150.
- **Window heights:** `windowHeights: [0.6, 0.57, 0.55, 0.59]` on both library types. These are shares of the
  catalogue storey grid (windows of 2.0, 1.8 and 1.65 m on storeys of 3.45, 3.3 and 2.8 m), with the ground storey
  assumed.
  - The planned `'diminishing'` (the canal survey medians, 0.72-0.76) drew every window about 0.5 m too tall on this row.
  - 148's own shares (0.57, 0.56, 0.64) put 147 over the 3,000-triangle front budget (3,018).
  - 151-152 and 150 keep the fitted default, which already matches the photo.
- **Relieving arches on storey 3** of 150 and 147: `windowHead {kind: 'relieving', storeys: [3], archRing {colour: stone,
  style: blocks, keystone}}`. Each window gets a white five-block ring with a keystone over a dark brick tympanum. 150's
  eared surrounds stay on storeys 0-2 (`surroundStoreys`); 147's already did.
- **Compile:** 19,441 triangles, all gates pass. The heaviest front is 147 at 2,970 triangles. The GLB audit's z-fight
  numbers are unchanged from the lane's build (2-3 cm overlap 60.4 down to 57.9 m²).

## White courses, springers, round arches and head lines (kit lane, 2026-10-14)

At gallery size four things on `kinker-2-30` and `nassau-145-152` were still wrong, and all four were kit work. Each one
is now a typed key in `intent.ts`, laid out in `fit.ts`, and marked in `oudWest.ts`. With the key absent, the output is
byte-identical.

| Key | Geometry |
|---|---|
| `palette.stoneMaterial: "painted"` (house palette) | Every stone-surface mesh is exported with `tintSource: measured`: bands, springers, keystones, rings, eared heads, console sills and quoins. The look then divides the colour by the stone cell mean (`cityMeasuredStoneTint`, 228/255) instead of `cityStoneTint`'s L ≥ 0.62 and 45 % mix into `#cfc8b8`. |
| `archRing.style: "springers"` + `springerSize: [length, height]` | No ring. A slab at each foot of the arch stands on the springing line and runs `length` outward from the arch edge, `height` tall, 6 cm proud. Two springers over a narrow pier are cut back to leave 0.09 m between them (`SPRINGER.gapM`). The keystone is cut at the eaves. |
| `archRing.shape: "round"` (relieving only) | A semicircle over the span (`floor(span / 2)`) instead of the 0.25 × span segment. |
| `archRing.springGap` (relieving only, m) | The springing line's height over the window head (default 0.04). |
| `windowHead.tympanum: "dark"` | The arch field over the springing line. It is filled in `palette.band`, or else in the wall colour × `DARK_TYMPANUM_K` (0.72: tympanum-to-wall luma measured at 0.67-0.80 on 150 and 147). It also works under a relieving arch. On a painted-brick house it is a measured tint like the wall. |
| `headLines` (per storey, needs a `windowHeights` list) | The window head sits at that share of its storey instead of the common 0.86 (`HEAD_LINE`). The sill follows from the window height. |
| `bandTransomAt` (`bands: painted`) | The transom band's centre, as a share of the window from the sill (default 0.78). |

**Measuring.** `palette-from-strip.ts` now prints `paint` (`stripPalette.ts paintFromFrames`). An overcast strip
measures white paint as a light grey: `#b2b3a8` on Kinkerstraat 8-10. The model's frames, though, are drawn near white.
`paint` takes the model's drawn frame colour and scales it by the photo's band-to-frame luma ratio, so the band reads
against the frames as it does in the photo. Run `--frame=white-painted` for the Nassaukade types.

## What the kit still cannot say

- **Windows:**
  - a T-window transom line set per house: the kit fixes it at 0.76, while the photo's top light is about the top 0.22;
  - band lines per storey: 147's storey-1 band runs at 0.69 of the window and its upper bands at the heads, and 149's
    storey-2 band runs at the head where its storey-1 band is at 0.65. The kit has one set of lines per front;
  - wedge-shaped springers and keystones: the slabs are square;
  - a dark recessed lintel course between the window head and the springing (150): the gap is plain wall;
  - painted dressings and a granite plinth on one house: there is one stone colour per house, so 150's springers take
    the frame paint.
- **Ornament and dormers:**
  - wreath medallions and cartouches between windows (151-152);
  - bracketed wooden cornices with modillions; the library cornice is one moulded run;
  - pediment dormers with tympanum ornament (`pediment` is a plain triangle; the photo shows a carved fan);
  - pointed dormers with fretwork.
- **Roofs and ground floors:**
  - bell-sided (curved) mansards; the kit only has straight-sided ones;
  - a souterrain with a side stoop and a granite plinth under a raised ground floor (150).
- **Balconies and erkers:**
  - ornamental cast-iron grilles; rails are bars only;
  - canted (three-sided) erker sides; `bayWindows` is box-sided.
- **Shopfronts:** awnings and signage (the corner bar).
