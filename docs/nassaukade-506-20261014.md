**Wall colour.** Each value is the median of the lit wall, with the darkest 20 % dropped. It is white-balanced against that
front's own white dressings, taken as a warm white #ebe5d5 because the strip has a golden-hour cast. Painted fronts use
`wallMaterial: 'painted-brick'`, so the measured colour reaches the game unclamped.

**Ground floors** are residential (BAG woonfunctie), except the corner bar at 151-152. Each has its door on the left.

## New kit components

Each one is typed in `intent.ts`, laid out in `fit.ts` and built in `oudWest.ts` or the library. With the key absent, the
output is byte-identical to before.

- **`bands: 'painted'`**, with `bandHeights {sill, transom, head}` in **metres** (defaults 0.14, 0.10 and 0.17), `bandLines`
  and `bandStoreys`. These are white stone-surface bands across the whole front, cut at a multi-bay erker:
  - the sill band sits with its top on the storey's lowest sill;
  - the transom band is centred at 0.78 of the window height;
  - the head band stands on the window heads.

  The key names are agreed with the Kinkerstraat lane: `bands: "painted"`, `bandHeights: {sill, head}` in metres, and
  `wallMaterial: "painted-brick"`.
- **`windowSurround: 'eared-keystone'`** is built as separate dressing slabs:
  - a head slab 0.10 m high and 0.08 m deep, running 0.12 m past each jamb;
  - an ear block (0.13 x 0.25 m) at each end;
  - `round(w / 0.4) - 1` keystone blocks.
- **`windowSills: 'consoles'`** is a sill slab (0.08 m high, 0.10 m proud, running 0.12 m past the jambs) on two consoles
  (0.10 x 0.16 m) under the jambs. Balcony bays are skipped.
- **`balconies.style: 'timber-bracketed'`** takes `rail: 'turned' | 'iron'`, `styleStoreys` and `storeyBays`.
  - **Platform:** 0.14 m thick, 0.75 m deep, its top at the storey floor, on 4-point profile brackets every 1.1 m.
  - **Guard:** 1.0 m high, with base and hand rails.
  - **Turned rail:** square-plan balusters every 0.16 m, each a shaft and a bulb, with the back and contact faces culled
    (12 triangles each).
  - **Iron rail:** bars every 0.14 m, in the door colour.
  - **`styleStoreys`** mixes timber platforms and library balconettes on one front.
  - **`storeyBays`** sets the bays per storey, for example s1 full width under a centre s2.
  - A 2 m turned balcony costs under 330 triangles.
- **`bayWindows.finish: 'painted'`** turns the erker's wall panels and cap to the trim colour and surface: the white timber
  erker.
- **`wallMaterial: 'painted-brick'`** gives the wall materials `tintSource: 'measured'`. Chunk buckets are keyed
  `<hex>_brick_measured` and the extra is exported, so the game skips the 0.3-0.6 lightness clamp.
- **The BAG erker chord** (`blockFace/bayOutline.ts`, run as `intake --bay-outline=<pand6>`) fixes 146. BAG draws that pand
  from above with its erker, which gave a 1.61 m "front".
  - The chord walks the ring past the bay's sides to the facade edges.
  - It clips the footprints and roofs and rebuilds the front facts, giving 6.17 m.
  - The uncorrected facts are kept in `facts-bay-outline.json`.
- **`mirrorFront`** now also mirrors `balconies.storeyBays`.

Window naming follows the canal-windows lane (`t-window`, `windowHeights`, `windowHead`, `windowSurround: 'sill'`). That lane,
and the Kinkerstraat types (`windowHead.tympanum`), are on `origin/main` but not on this branch. Merging `origin/main` was
refused by the session's permission classifier, twice. A dry run (`git merge-tree`) shows conflicts in `fit.ts` and
`intent.ts` only. After the merge, the face should switch to:

- `windows: 't-window'` and `windowHeights: 'diminishing'`;
- `windowHead` tympanum or relieving on s3 of 150 and 147.

Two review checks fail for known reasons:

- **Walls** on 148, 146 and 145 read slightly greyer than the strip (chroma -0.11 to -0.17). Part of that is the strip's
  golden cast, so the model is left at the balanced median.
- **Gable peaks** fail on 149, 146 and 145, because the photo detector counts the pointed dormers as gables.

## What the kit still cannot say

- **Waiting on the windows-lane merge:**
  - T-windows and diminishing window heights;
  - arched tympana or relieving arches over flat heads (150 and 147, s3).
- **Ornament and dormers:**
  - wreath medallions and cartouches between windows (151-152);
  - bracketed wooden cornices with modillions; the library cornice is one moulded run;
  - pediment dormers with tympanum ornament (`pediment` is a plain triangle; the photo shows a carved fan);
  - pointed dormers with fretwork.
- **Roofs and ground floors:**
  - bell-sided (curved) mansards; the kit only has straight-sided ones;
  - a souterrain with a side stoop and a granite plinth under a raised ground floor (150).
- **Balconies and erkers:**
  - ornamental cast-iron grilles; rails are bars only;
  - canted (three-sided) erker sides; `bayWindows` is box-sided.
- **Shopfronts:** awnings and signage (the corner bar).
